Kairo is a private, fully-offline planner that brings tasks, a calendar, habits, and time tracking together in one place — with no account, no sign-in, and no network permission at all.

Private by construction. The app declares no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. Everything stays on your device. The only permissions it uses are local: notifications and exact alarms for on-device reminders, and vibrate. Your database can optionally be encrypted at rest (AES-256 via SQLCipher, with the key held in the Android KeyStore).

No lock-in. Your data is yours: lossless JSON export and import, plus Markdown and CSV export. Back up to a folder you choose, or move to another device, with nothing held hostage behind a subscription.

What's inside:

• Tasks and outlining — nested subtasks with unlimited depth, folders and lists, priorities (simple, or advanced importance/urgency), due and start dates, deadlines, tags, contexts, checklists, and natural-language quick capture.

• Smart lists — Inbox, Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 Days, Do Next (a computed priority ranking), Scheduled, Flagged, Waiting-on, Completed, and Trash, all with live counts.

• Calendar — list, day, 3-day, week, month, and year views, drag-to-reschedule, and interactive time-blocking.

• Habits — build or quit habits with flexible schedules, numeric or checkbox check-ins, streaks, reminders, and per-habit analytics.

• Time tracking — one timeline that links tasks and habits to the time you actually spend, with goals and reports.

• Reminders — local exact alarms with Done and Snooze actions, an optional daily summary, and rescheduling after reboot.

• Views and polish — Eisenhower matrix, workload forecast, weekly review, home-screen widgets, a quick-settings tile, light/dark/AMOLED themes, Material You, any week-start, 12/24h clock, and any time zone.

Native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose (Material 3), Room/SQLite. Free, offline, and yours.
