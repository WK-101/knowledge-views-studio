# Parley glossary

One name per concept, used the same way in every screen, menu, notification and setting. English strings use UK spelling ("favourite", "colour", "optimisation") and "email" without a hyphen. When a string names a place in the app, it uses the exact title that place shows.

See also [WRITING.md](WRITING.md) for voice and tone, and [SETTINGS.md](SETTINGS.md) for where each setting lives.

## People

| Term | What it means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Favourites** | People you call often and want one tap away: the starred contacts. They're the Favourites tab (or the section at the top of Contacts), the widget, and the "loud favourites" option in blocking. Android stores this as the contact's star, so other apps see it too. | Not "Starred", not "Favorites". Starring someone and adding them to Favourites are the same action. |
| **Frequent** | A section of Favourites, not a separate list: the people you call most lately, picked by Parley from your call history. It's automatic, and you don't choose who appears. Always under the favourites (the Favourites tab, and Favourites in Contacts with "Show Frequent"). | Not a list you edit, and never a place of its own. |
| **Circle** | People you want to keep in touch with, each with a rhythm ("every 2 weeks") and gentle reminders. The one opt-in layer: it stays empty, and adds nothing to other screens, until you add someone. Always capitalised: "your Circle", "Add to your Circle". | Not a group and not Favourites: being in the Circle doesn't star anyone. Its suggestions are "Suggested for your Circle", never "Frequent". |
| **Labels** | Groups of contacts you make yourself ("Family", "Work"), for organising: filter by them, message a whole label, give one a ringtone. Android calls them groups; Parley says labels everywhere. | Not "groups" or "tags". |
| **To call** | A section of Recents, not a separate place: the calls you said you'd make (Remind me, follow-ups) and missed calls you haven't returned, as a strip at the top of Recents that opens the full list. Its reminder and Remind me's confirmation lead there. | Not a tab or a menu item. Not "callbacks" or "Call later". |
| **Private contacts** | Contacts kept only inside Parley, encrypted, invisible to other apps. Private is a variant of a contact, not another kind: the same page, editor and features, a lock on the photo in lists, the chip "Private" on the page (TalkBack: "Private · hidden from other apps"), and **Make private** / **Make visible to other apps** to convert ([CONTACT_MODEL.md](CONTACT_MODEL.md)), one contact or a selection alike. | Not "vault" in the interface. Not "Move to private". |
| **Hide private contacts** | The switch (Settings › Privacy & security, and the Quick Settings tile) that takes private contacts and their calls out of every list and search until it's turned off. A duress session turns it on whatever the switch says. | Not "discreet mode" in the interface (code and older notes call it that). |
| **Name in their language** | A contact's name in their own language and script, kept beside the everyday name: "Иван Петров" for Ivan Petrov. Shown under the name on the page and the call screen (not in lists). Other apps see it as a nickname labelled "Name in Russian"; a nickname the person labelled "Name in …" themselves stays a nickname. **Add an English spelling** turns a name typed in another script into this, with its Latin spelling as the main name. | Not "native name", "original name" or "alias" in the interface. Not the phonetic name (how a name is said). |
| **Languages** | The languages someone speaks, in order: the first is the one to use with them. The page says "Speaks Russian, English"; the filter chip says "Speaks Russian". | Not "Preferred language" for the list as a whole. |
| **Citizenship** | The countries someone is a citizen of, one or several. Shown only on their page (More), never on a call screen. The filter chip says "Citizen of Portugal". | Not "Nationality". Not a place they live: that's Country. |
| **Temporary contacts** | Contacts, private or not, that delete themselves after a time you choose. Also a variant: the chip "Temporary · 5 days left" (TalkBack: "deletes itself on …"), and **Keep permanently** to undo it. Made with **Delete automatically…**, the one name on the contact page, in the selection menu and in "Settings for this contact" (on a contact that already deletes itself, it changes when). When the time is up Parley asks once before deleting ("Ask before deleting temporary contacts", on by default). | Not "Delete after…" or "Change auto-delete". |
| **Archived** | A contact kept by Parley but out of the way: gone from the contact lists, search, pickers, widgets and other apps (Parley moves it out of Android's address book), yet Parley still names their calls (call screen, Recents, missed calls, history), finds them with Search everything, and treats them as a saved contact (screening, Hide private contacts, a duress session). Made with the contact page's **Kept as** row (Visible · Private · Archived), **Archive instead** in the Delete question, or **Archive them** in the Health check's stale group; an archived contact opens a read-only page with **Unarchive**, and Contacts search says "Also archived: Ana · Show"; listed in Contacts › ⋮ › **Archived**, with **Unarchive**, which puts the contact back in the account it came from (or asks which). Travels in backups (with its note for calls, agenda, Circle and moments) and exports, marked as archived and archived again when imported from Parley's encrypted export (a plain file imports them as ordinary contacts). Photos are kept whole. Archiving also takes the contact out of the account it is saved in, so it leaves the person's other devices; Undo puts it back. Many contacts can be archived at once from the Contacts selection (⋮ › Privacy… › Archive). A variant like Private and Temporary, but nothing is locked. | Not "Hide", "Private" or "Delete". Archived contacts aren't hidden behind a lock. |
| **Chapter** | A label given an end, for a period of life (a house move, a hospital stay, a trip, a project): **Give it an end** on the label's page, on a date or after some weeks or months. The label shows what is left ("3 weeks left"). When it ends, Parley asks once (a notification and a card on the label): **Keep the label as it is**, **Archive** who joined it during the chapter, **Remove the label** (contacts stay), or **Delete** the temporary contacts made for it, with Undo. Nothing happens by itself. A shared label's chapter is decided on the phone that started it. | Not "expiring label" or "project". |
| **Relationship status** | The line under a contact's name from their relations: "Married to Sam", "Partner of Alex". **Ex-spouse** has no Android type, so it is stored as the custom label "Ex-spouse" (other apps and vCards show that text) and ends the "Married to" line. "Ex-wife" and "Ex-husband" are their own types and end it too. Labels are recognised in English ("ex-spouse", "ex-wife", "ex-husband"); one typed in another language in another app shows as written and doesn't change the line. Month and country names are matched in English, the phone's language and each country's own languages. | Not "marital status". |
| **My card** | Your own card, kept by Parley as a whole contact: every field and option a contact has (name parts, numbers, emails, addresses, dates, websites, work, relations, languages, citizenship, note, photo), edited with the same editor. Shared as a QR code or vCard with the parts you tick under **What your card includes** (name, numbers and email until you choose; never the note or relations unless ticked), and used for "Send my details". | Not "My details" or "My profile". |
| **Relation** | How someone is related to a contact ("Wife: Sam"). Parley shows the **other side** on Sam's contact: the opposite word, by the gender the holder's **pronouns** say ("Husband" when Alex is he/him, "Wife" for she/her, "Spouse" otherwise; never guessed from a name). A custom label shows as typed on its own side and as "Related" on the other. **Change how this shows** corrects the other side, and Parley remembers it for the two of them. | Not "link" or "connection". |

### Which one?

Each has one job, so a person can be in several without it meaning the same thing twice:

- **Favourites:** who you want one tap away (you choose; the star).
- **Frequent:** who you actually call most (Parley notices; shown under Favourites).
- **Circle:** who you want to keep in touch with (opt-in; a rhythm and reminders).
- **Labels:** how you organise everyone (you name them; Family, Work).
- **To call:** calls you owe right now (in Recents; done once you call).

### Favourites and Do Not Disturb

A label's **Allow through Do Not Disturb** works by starring its members, because Android only lets starred contacts through Do Not Disturb. Starred contacts *are* Favourites, so turning it on adds those people to Favourites. Parley doesn't hide this: the confirmation lists every person who will be starred ("These 4 people will be starred and appear in Favourites") before anything changes. The switch's summary says the same. "Star N new members" asks with the same list. Turning it off unstars only the people Parley starred for that label.

## App lock

| Term | What it means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Parley PIN** | The app lock's own PIN, used instead of the phone's fingerprint or screen lock (Settings › Privacy & security › App lock › Unlock with). | Not "passcode" or "app password". |
| **Duress PIN** | A second PIN that opens Parley looking normal, with private contacts and sensitive notes hidden until the Parley PIN is used again. | Not "panic PIN", "decoy" or "fake PIN". |

## Reaching someone

| Term | What it means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Message or call on…** | The one sheet, and the one menu name, for reaching a number through an app: **Call** (through the phone) first, then **Message on** (chat apps, SMS), then **Call on** (voice and video calls in apps). The same sheet opens for saved contacts, private contacts and unsaved numbers. The contact page's section of the same name uses the same rows. | Not "Message on…", "Reach via apps" or "Messengers". |
| **Usual** | The way Parley remembers for a person (their usual chat app, call app or video app). Long-press a button to make it the usual one. | Not "default" or "preferred" in the interface. |

## Calls

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Recents** | The tab with your calls. |
| **Call history** | The calls themselves: Android's call log, and Parley's own encrypted copy. Two settings, named for what they act on: **Keep Parley's copy of calls** (the copy, on or off) and **Trim Android's call log** (deletes older calls from Android's log once you choose a limit; the copy keeps to the same limit). Not "Keep full call history" and "Keep call history", which read as the same thing. |
| **From the network** | The tag beside a name the mobile network sent with a call (caller name presentation, such as India's CNAP) for a number you haven't saved: in Recents, on the number's page, in Search everything, and in the missed-call notification's line. Only while **Remember names from the network** is on (Settings › Calls › Answering, off by default): Parley then keeps the latest name per number (and the one before when it changes) on this phone only. Under a saved contact's name it shows as a small "Network: …" line on the call screen and the number's page, when it is a different name; it is what the network says, never a verdict on who is calling. A saved name always wins, and a private contact's number never gets one. Turning the setting off asks whether to delete the names kept (Delete · Keep for later). Not "caller ID name", "CNAP" or "network ID" in the interface. |
| **Call insights** | Talk time, top people and calls you didn't return. Always "Call insights", never plain "Insights". |
| **Blocking & screening** | The rules that decide which calls ring. The screen, its row in Tools and the Settings row all use this name. "Blocking & spam" is the Settings *category* that holds it and the spam lists. |
| **Expecting a call** | Lets unknown numbers ring for a while (a delivery, a callback). |
| **SIMs and calling abroad** | Settings › Calls › SIMs & carrier › the SIM screen: your SIMs and the abroad settings. **Plan minutes per SIM** (billing and a warning) open from Tools; the SIM screen shows them once a SIM has a plan. |
| **Sales lines (your calls)** | Numbers your own calls suggest are sales lines: one choice, Off · Tag quietly · Tag and silence. |
| **Situations** | One tap sets a moment: **Driving**, **Meeting**, **Night**, **Travelling** and the ones you make. Each sets who may ring, the reply offered first, the speaker, auto-answer, the drive profile's switches, the abroad help and the SIM for calls; turning it off puts back what you had. One is on at a time; it can turn on by itself at set times, when your car (or a Bluetooth device) connects or when you're abroad. Turned on by hand, it asks how long: For 1 hour, Until [a time], or Until I turn it off. While one lets only some people ring, a silent notice says so, with Turn off. On Settings › Calls › Situations (the page that also holds helpers, the drive profile, phone menus and talk-time reminders and limits), the Quick Settings tile "Situation" and a line on the home screen while one is on ("Night is on · Turn off"). Not "mode", "profile" or "focus". |
| **Case file** | What Parley keeps per organisation you deal with (a bank, an insurer, a council): your calls with them, how long you waited on hold, the menu keys you pressed, the reference numbers they gave you and open promises, with **Export as PDF** for a complaint. Kept by itself for a saved organisation, or with ⋮ › **Keep a case file** for anyone (a company's at the top of ⋮, a person's under More…). During a call, **Keep as a reference** offers digits you typed but never fills them in, and never offers a PIN or a card number. A private contact's case hides with private contacts, including for a contact made private after its case began. **Stop keeping** stays stopped, even after restoring an older backup. Each case has a status, **Open · Waiting for them · Resolved**, set with one tap on its page and printed in the PDF. Tools › **Case files** lists them all, the newest activity first, resolved ones last; its ⋮ › **Stop keeping case files for all** stops every case and keeps organisations' calls from starting new ones (Keep a case file still works; **Start again** lets organisations start them again). |
| **Rescue call** | A call that looks real, on Parley's own call screen, to help you leave somewhere: you choose who "calls" (a name such as "Mum", or a contact, whose call then shows with their photo, ringtone and vibration) and when (now, in 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or at a time). It isn't a call: nothing is dialled and nothing is kept (not in Recents, notes, statistics, case files, Recall or backups), and a real call always comes first and ends it. On Settings › Calls › Situations, in Tools, as the launcher shortcut "Rescue call" and with a long press on the Situation tile (Parley's other tiles keep App info on a long press); never on the home screen. A call set before the phone restarted, or long past its time, can't ring: the screen then says "No rescue call is waiting". Not "fake call" in the interface. |
| **To talk about** | Things to raise with someone the next time you speak (the agenda). Each is a promise you owe yourself, kept as a `[ ]` line of their **Note for calls** (for a number that isn't saved, of the notes on that number), so ticking one off is ticking that promise. Added with **Add something to talk about** on a contact's page or a number's page, from Recall (a result's ⋮), from text shared to Parley, and during a call (More). Shown under the caller when you call them or they call you (the first two, **Show more** for the rest; on the lock screen only how many, unless notes may show there), in the pre-call peek, and after the call as **Did you cover these?** with **All covered** and **Keep for next time**. A private contact's show only while private contacts are unlocked. Recall lists them as "To talk about"; case files list them with the open promises. |

## Undo

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **History & undo** | The one place to get something back, one row in Settings › Backup & sync. It has three tabs: **Contacts** (contacts deleted, edited, merged or separated in Parley, 30 days; deleted private contacts are kept sealed there and listed after unlocking), **Calls** (calls deleted in Parley, 30 days) and **Snapshots** (daily snapshots of the address book, 6 months). Messages that say where to restore something name this place ("You can restore it from History & undo"). |
| **Version history** | One contact's earlier versions, on its page. |

## Navigation

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Tools** | The one hub: everything Parley does, grouped by the job you want done ("Stop spam", "Never lose a contact"…), each row opening the feature, the most used first and the rest under "n more"; searchable. Lock now (with the app lock) and Expecting a call work right on it. Reached from every tab's ⋮ menu, the top of Settings and the What's new card. Its rows come from `CapabilityCatalog`; every screen a row can open has its row, and every headline feature of a release has a row tagged with that release (the What's new card names up to three of them). A feature that lives on each label or contact (Chapters, To talk about, the family spam shield) has a row that opens its help page, which leads to the place. **Help & troubleshooting** is its own group, last. Settings holds preferences, Tools holds tools: Settings has no row that only opens a tool (search still finds the tool). Not "What Parley can do" (its old name, which search still finds). The Privacy dashboard lives under Settings › Privacy & security. |
| **Reminders** | The Settings page (a row of the Settings list) with every kind of reminder Parley sends (missed calls again, To call and follow-ups, keep in touch, birthdays and dates, backups, temporary contacts that are due), each with its switch and time (To call is a link to its own list); also the notification channel group that holds most of their channels. Missed calls, backup problems (a failed scheduled backup) and temporary contacts keep channels outside the group, so turning it off never hides them. Not "Notifications", which is Settings › Notifications & device. |
| **Coming from another phone?** | Where to export contacts, call history and block lists on the old phone, each opening Parley's own importer. Onboarding's optional last step, and in Tools. |
| **⋮ (More options)** | At most seven items, only the tab's own, then Tools and Settings. Every other menu and action sheet keeps to seven too, most used first; the rarer actions sit under **Share…**, **Privacy…**, **More…** or **Allow, report…**, each a sheet of its own. |
| **Coming from another phone?** | Where to export contacts, call history and block lists on the old phone, each opening Parley's own importer; first, **Restore a Parley backup** for someone moving from another phone with Parley. Onboarding's optional last step, and in Tools. The first run's welcome also offers **From another phone with Parley? Restore a backup**: the default phone app and permissions follow, then the backup screen, skipping Set up the basics (which a restore would overwrite). |
| **Help & troubleshooting** | Tools' last group: short task pages for what people ask a phone app ("Parley's call screen doesn't show", "A call didn't ring", "A contact disappeared after Archive"…), each ending in one button to the place that fixes it (Default phone app, Test a call, Archived contacts…). Nothing in it is a setting. Not "FAQ" or "Support". |
| **⋮ (More options)** | At most seven items, only the tab's own, then Tools and Settings. Every other menu and action sheet keeps to seven too, most used first; the rarer actions sit under **Share…**, **Privacy…**, **More…** or **Why it rang…**, each a sheet of its own. |
| **Long-press** | Selects, in every list that has a selection (Contacts, Recents); the selection bar's ⋮ then has the row's actions. A list without a selection gives each row a trailing ⋮. On buttons and tiles that act at once, a long-press offers the alternative (Call → Call with a reason, Message → Message or call on…, a Favourites tile → its page, keypad 2–9 → speed dial). |
| **Explainers** | One-line tips at a concept's first appearance (Private, Temporary, Circle, Labels, Favourites with Frequent, History & undo, To call), in the words of this glossary; each shows once. Each says what sets its concept apart from its neighbours (Circle: "doesn't star anyone"; Favourites: Frequent is under them). |
| **Contact health check** | The screen that finds numbers without a country code, numbers that seem out of service (on more than one day the network said the number isn't allocated; never for calls placed while roaming to a number saved without its country code, in airplane mode, or that just failed fast; nothing changes unless you choose, and **Move to note**'s undo puts back every row it removed), and empty or stale contacts. Not "Tidy up". |
| **Search everything** | The Contacts search's chip that widens it to everything Parley remembers (Recall): calls with their dates, notes, promises, notes after calls, chats opened from Parley, deleted contacts, snapshots and number memory. It understands plain date and call words ("plumber march", "who called yesterday"), and runs by itself when the contacts give nothing. The Recents search has it too: it runs by itself when no call matches ("No calls match, but Parley remembers"), and **Search everything for "…"** at the end of the calls turns it on. The Tools row and the launcher shortcut **Search everything** open the Recents search with it on. Not a tab or a screen of its own. |

## Words to avoid

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Favorites, favorite | Favourites, favourite |
| e-mail, E-mail | email, Email |
| Recently deleted (as a place), Recently deleted & changed, Recently deleted calls | History & undo (its Calls tab) |
| Discreet mode | Hide private contacts |
| Move to private | Make private |
| Delete after…, Change auto-delete | Delete automatically… |
| Keep full call history, Keep call history | Keep Parley's copy of calls, Trim Android's call log |
| Log interaction | Log a chat or visit |
| What changed, time machine (as a title) | Snapshots (tab), Daily snapshots (setting) |
| Insights | Call insights |
| Blocked numbers (for the rules screen) | Blocking & screening ("Blocked numbers (system list)" is Android's own list) |
| Message on…, Reach via apps, Messengers | Message or call on… |
| your circle | your Circle |
